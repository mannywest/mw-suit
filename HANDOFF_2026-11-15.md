## Things found (so you do not redo them)
- The "dark flaps" at the front sides of the neck are the steep slope of the body rising to the neck, seen from behind = its inside (back faces, render dark). Not a defect; only the ragged top edge needed smoothing.
- The thin black line across the neck opening in the TOP view is the overlapped front closure edge near the button (z ~ +183..206 mm, y ~ 1.0-1.15 m), seen straight down through the open front. Not a neck flaw; hidden by shirt/waistcoat in the app. Removing slivers there changed nothing.
- Rim rebuild by phi (angle) works for |phi| <= ~100 deg. Past that the rim runs steeply down the front and the angle-ordered zipper stitch opened new holes (tried 122, 152 deg, with and without a radius floor). Use edge relaxation (base_e2.py) there instead, or an arc-length based stitch.
- Ear-clipping a boundary loop to fill body holes made huge overlapping triangles; hidden patches 1.2 mm under the surface (peak_step2d.py make_hole_patches) worked.
- Wider body-lift feather around the neck made hole counts worse (170 / 316 pixels); keep the small one.
- Coordinates: neck axis x=0, z=-0.005 (ZC); phi = atan2(x, -(z-ZC)) in degrees, 0 = back centre, +-180 = front. Weave: TEXCOORD_1 about 33.8 units/m in x, -33.5 units/m in y.
- The Jacket mesh now has primitives: Jacket_Wool (body), Trousers_Wool (406 tris at y 0.95-1.0, x +-0.13; leave alone). Other meshes: Buttons, Shirt, Tie, Trim, Trousers, Waistcoat_Wool.
